BANJOFY SONG ANALYSIS LABORATORY 017 — WINDOWS TEST
ALTERNATIVE BEAT-GRID AUDITION

PURPOSE
Tennessee Waltz showed that meter, phase and simple half-pulse changes cannot repair an incorrect beat grid. Build 017 creates four genuinely different beat timestamp sets directly from the original audio.

METHODS
1. Standard full mix
2. Percussion focused
3. Low-frequency rhythm
4. Steady slow pulse

TEST
1. Run BanjofySongAnalysisLab017.exe.
2. Select Tennessee Waltz.
3. Click Create Alternative Beat Grids and wait for completion.
4. Click Play Alternative Beat Grids.
5. Test every method. Each selection restarts from the beginning.
6. Listen only to whether the identical ordinary clicks follow the genuine waltz beat. There are no strong downbeat clicks in this test.

REPORT
- Which method follows the real beat most closely?
- Does it remain aligned without speeding up, slowing down or drifting?
- Does the displayed chord still change at the correct audible moment?
- Are any methods obviously double-time or half-time?

CHECK FILENAME ONLY
Analysis\<song-id>\alternative_beat_standard.wav
Analysis\<song-id>\alternative_beat_percussive.wav
Analysis\<song-id>\alternative_beat_low_frequency.wav
Analysis\<song-id>\alternative_beat_steady_pulse.wav
Analysis\<song-id>\alternative_beat_grids.json

Do not confirm meter or phase until one underlying beat grid passes musically.
