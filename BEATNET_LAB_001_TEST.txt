BANJOFY BEATNET LISTENING LABORATORY 001 — WINDOWS TEST

This is a completely separate timing experiment.
It does not use Banjofy's old timing scores or the verified answers.

1. Open the BanjofyBeatNetLab001 folder.
2. Start BanjofyBeatNetLab001.exe from inside that folder.
3. Choose one of the four test songs.
4. Keep All three trained models selected.
5. Choose an empty output folder.
6. Click Run BeatNet Listening Test.
7. Wait for all three models to finish.

For each model open the file ending:
__CLICKED_SONG.wav

The HIGH click is Beat 1.
The lower click marks the other beats.

Listen from the beginning and again near the end.
A model passes only when:
- every normal click follows the musical beat;
- the high click lands on the real first beat of each bar;
- the clicks remain locked throughout the song;
- the reported meter is 3/4 or 4/4 correctly;
- the reported BPM is the musician-counted BPM rather than half-time or double-time.

Run all four songs. Keep the TXT reports and clicked WAV files.
