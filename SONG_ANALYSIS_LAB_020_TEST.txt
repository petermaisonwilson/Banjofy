WHY THIS BUILD IS DIFFERENT
Chord changes are allowed on Beat 1, Beat 2 or Beat 3.
Chord-change proximity contributes only weakly to beat-grid quality.
It has zero weight when choosing the downbeat phase.

Build 020 instead scores:
- repeating Beat-1 accent across complete bars
- bar-to-bar accent-pattern consistency
- recurring lift into Beat 1 from the preceding beat
- beat-grid stability
- low-frequency rhythmic support

SAFETY
The recommendation must not alter the already confirmed meter, beat grid or
phase.
