BANJOFY SONG ANALYSIS LABORATORY 010 — WINDOWS TEST

Scope: 3/4 and 4/4 only.

1. Run the EXE and select the analysed song.
2. Click Detect 3/4 or 4/4 and Create Audible Check.
3. Click Play 180-Second Audible + Visual Check.
4. Confirm the high click/ordinary marker follows each beat.
5. Confirm the low click/DOWNBEAT marker follows each bar start.
6. Judge the intro, established song and any drift through the three-minute test.
7. Report meter result, best candidate, rhythmic-section confidence, full-track confidence, and confidence window.

Check by filename only:
- audible_bar_check.wav
- song_structure.json

Open the existing JSON files in Notepad and inspect their contents for the new confidence-window fields.
